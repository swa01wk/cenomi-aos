How Quick Quote works

  1. The agent writes a brief with you and confirms it.
  2. Sourcing picks suppliers and sends requests for quotation.
  3. Quotes are compared; Sourcing selects one.
  4. Procurement approves the purchase order.
  5. You get a handover with the supplier and delivery date.
What you'll see Your brief, progress through each stage, and the outcome. Supplier prices stay with Sourcing and Procurement.
What the agent can do Read, draft and ask questions. Every email or ERP update is approved by a named person first.

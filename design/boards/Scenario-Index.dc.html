AOS POC · platform screens

The Console and the Workspace, shown through one example run

These are AOS platform screens. They work the same for any digital worker and any domain pack. To show them in motion, the walkthrough follows one example run from the procurement_demo pack: Quick Quote QQ-0001, 40 cleaning trolleys. Seventeen stops cover building the digital worker, running one job across three people and governing it afterwards. Every screen carries a bar like this one naming the screen, what happens in the example and what the kernel did.

6 · What the platform builds, what the pack supplies

A different scenario is a different domain pack on the same kernel and the same screens. A second, smaller pack is a stretch goal in M5.
Built once, in the platform
  • Every Console screen: organization, packs, Seats, workflows, policies, connectors, instances, approvals, observability, audit, kill switch
  • The Workspace shell, My jobs, New job, team queues and the inbox
  • The job page: conversation, work panel, pending items and the stage stepper
  • Decision and approval cards, multi-step approval chains
  • Document views by type: document, sheet, tracker, message composer, record preview
  • Refusal, conflict, error and loading states; tokens and components
Supplied by the domain pack (example values)
  • Workflow names, stages and who may start them (Quick Quote)
  • The Seat, its Skills and their prompts (the Sourcing Agent)
  • Document types, their fields and which view shows each (brief, RFQ email, comparison)
  • Who may see which field (a requester never sees prices)
  • Policies and approval chains (two-step PO approval above SAR 25,000)
  • Connectors (mock mail, mock ERP) and team queues (Sourcing)

Names, figures and dates in the example run are illustrative. Connectors are the POC's mock mail and mock ERP; the demo's fault switches seed the budget leak at stop 7 and the ERP timeout at stop 13.

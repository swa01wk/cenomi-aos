Quick Quote

Published v1
Find suppliers, collect quotes and raise a purchase order. Started by a requester from New job; run by the sourcing_agent role.
  1. 1 · initial Intake request_intake
    ✓ brief_schema     → on decision.brief_confirmed by requester
  2. 2 · join Shortlisting No Task; waits for the buyer and the discovery branch → on decision.shortlist_confirmed by buyer, if shortlist_count >= 3
  3. 3 RFQ drafting rfq_draft · rfq_authoring
    ✓ leak_check     → on task.completed.rfq_draft
  4. 4 · leaves AOS RFQ sending rfq_send · rfq_authoring
    ✓ recipient_check · needs approval     → on task.completed.rfq_send; starts timer quote_deadline
  5. 5 · event-driven Awaiting quotes on mail.message_received:
    quote_triage, one per reply     → on timer.quotes_closed or event.all_quotes_received, if quote_count >= 2
  6. 6 Comparison quote_comparison
    ✓ score_recompute     → on decision.supplier_selected by buyer; locks the comparison
  7. 7 · leaves AOS Ordering po_create · quote_comparison
    ✓ payload_match · needs approval     → on task.completed.po_create; handover, Outcome Record, timers cancelled
  8. 8 · final Closed No exits. Records stay; nothing can reopen it.
parallel branch · discovery Starts supplier_lookup (✓ sources_cited) when intake raises event.intent_identified. Joins at Shortlisting, all required, only if both sides used the same brief_version.
timer · quote_deadline 5 days after the RFQs go out (15 minutes on the demo clock). Emits quotes_closed exactly once.
Amber Tasks commit effects outside AOS; the policy quick_quote_gates requires an approval before each one runs.

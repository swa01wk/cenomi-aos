Lifecycle a Seat goes through

  1. DRAFT
  2. →
  3. CONFIGURED
  4. →
  5. TESTING
  6. →
  7. APPROVED
  8. →
  9. ACTIVE
  • Configured needs all six configuration checks; Testing needs both eval suites to pass.
  • Only the Seat owner activates, and never the person who configured it.
  • From Active: Paused (owner, reversible) or Killed (emergency, needs a review to lift). Retired ends it.

# Master RFP authoring

Turn the confirmed brief into a **master RFP** document for executive review before invitations go out.

1. Read the brief and shortlisted suppliers from the job snapshot.
2. Draft a substantial `master_rfp`: use multiple paragraphs in scope and requirements (not bullet stubs). Include title, scope summary, functional and technical requirements, delivery timeline, proposal deadline (from the brief), and evaluation criteria (price, delivery, relevant experience, support — plain language).
3. After save, the CDO (`procurement_approver`) reviews and may edit the document in the work panel before approving; only then does the buyer send vendor invitations.
4. Do not include internal `budget_guidance` in shareable fields; keep it only on the brief if present.
5. Save one `master_rfp` draft keyed `main`. Use `leak_check_diff` before finishing if the pack expects it.
